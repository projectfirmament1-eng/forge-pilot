# Device Compatibility & Web3 Wallet Requirements

## WatchSkins-Compatible Devices

### Apple Watch
- Requirements: watchOS 7.0 or later

### Wear OS
- Requirements: Wear OS 3.0 or later

### Garmin
- Requirements: Compatible Garmin smartwatches

### Samsung Galaxy Watch
- Requirements: Galaxy Watch 4 or later

## Web3 Wallet Requirements
- Supports WalletConnect protocol
- Minimum balance for gas fees
- Supports ERC-721 and ERC-1155 standards
