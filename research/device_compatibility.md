# WatchSkins Device Compatibility

## Supported Devices

### Apple Watch
- Series 4 and newer
- Requires WatchOS 7.0+

### Wear OS
- All Wear OS 3.0+ devices

### Garmin
- Garmin Smartwatches with Connect IQ 4.0+

### Samsung Galaxy Watch
- Galaxy Watch 4 and newer

## Web3 Wallet Requirements

- Compatible with Ethereum and EVM-based chains
- Smart contract wallet support required
- ENS name resolution support
